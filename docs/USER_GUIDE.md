## 1. What You Need Before You Start

Before you can place a bet, you need two things:

1. **A Freighter wallet** — a free browser extension that holds your funds and signs transactions. Download it from [freighter.app](https://www.freighter.app/).
2. **Some funds on the supported network** — you'll need the network's native token to place bets and pay small transaction fees.

> 💡 **Tip:** Never share your wallet's secret recovery phrase with anyone. The app will never ask for it.

---

## 2. Connecting Your Wallet

Connecting your wallet lets the app see your account so you can place bets and view your positions. Your funds always stay in your wallet — the app never takes custody of them.

### Step 1 — Install Freighter

If you haven't already, install the Freighter browser extension and create a wallet. Follow the on-screen instructions to back up your recovery phrase somewhere safe.

![Step 1 — Install Freighter](./images/install-freighter.png)

### Step 2 — Open the app

Visit the app in your browser. Make sure the Freighter extension is unlocked (click the Freighter icon in your browser toolbar and enter your password if prompted).

![Step 2 — Unlock Freighter](./images/unlock-freighter.png)

### Step 3 — Click "Connect Wallet"

In the top-right corner of the app, click the **Connect Wallet** button.

![Step 3 — Connect Wallet button](./images/connect-wallet.png)

### Step 4 — Approve the connection

Freighter will pop up asking you to approve the connection. Review the request and click **Approve**.

![Step 4 — Approve in Freighter](./images/approve-connection.png)

### Step 5 — Confirm you're connected

Once connected, the button will show a shortened version of your wallet address (for example, `GABC…XYZ`). You're now connected and ready to bet!

![Step 5 — Connected state](./images/wallet-connected.png)

> 💡 **Tip:** You can disconnect at any time by clicking your address and choosing **Disconnect**.

## 3. Understanding Odds

Odds tell you how likely the market thinks an outcome is, and how much you could win if you're right.

### What the odds mean

Odds are shown as a **percentage** (for example, **65%**) and sometimes as a **multiplier** (for example, **1.54x**).

- A **65%** chance means the market believes there's roughly a 65 in 100 chance that outcome will happen.
- The **multiplier** tells you how much you'd get back per unit you bet if you win. A **1.54x** multiplier means a winning bet of 100 returns about 154 (your 100 back plus 54 profit).

### Reading the odds display

Each market shows the odds for every possible outcome. The higher the percentage, the more likely the market thinks that outcome is — and the smaller your potential payout per unit bet.

![Odds display](./images/odds-display.png)

### The odds bar

The colored bar across the top of a market shows the relative odds of each outcome at a glance. A larger colored segment means a more likely outcome.

![Market odds bar](./images/market-odds-bar.png)

> 💡 **Tip:** Odds change as more people bet. The odds you see when you confirm your bet are the odds you get.

## 4. Placing a Bet

Placing a bet takes just a few clicks. Make sure your wallet is connected first (see [Section 2](#2-connecting-your-wallet)).

### Step 1 — Choose a market

Browse the list of open markets and click the one you want to bet on.

![Step 1 — Choose a market](./images/choose-market.png)

### Step 2 — Pick your outcome

Select the outcome you think will happen (for example, **Yes** or **No**).

![Step 2 — Pick an outcome](./images/pick-outcome.png)

### Step 3 — Enter your amount

Type how much you want to bet in the amount field. You'll see a preview of your potential payout based on the current odds.

![Step 3 — Enter amount](./images/enter-amount.png)

### Step 4 — Review and confirm

Click **Place Bet**. A confirmation window will appear showing:

- The outcome you selected
- Your bet amount
- The current odds
- Your potential payout

Review the details carefully, then click **Confirm**.

![Step 4 — Confirm bet](./images/confirm-bet.png)

### Step 5 — Approve in Freighter

Freighter will pop up asking you to sign the transaction. Review it and click **Approve**.

![Step 5 — Approve transaction](./images/approve-transaction.png)

### Step 6 — Wait for confirmation

Your bet will be submitted to the network. Within a few seconds you'll see a success message and your bet will appear in your portfolio.

![Step 6 — Bet confirmed](./images/bet-confirmed.png)

> ⚠️ **Note:** Once a bet is confirmed on the network it cannot be cancelled. Double-check your amount and outcome before approving.

## 5. Viewing Your Portfolio

Your portfolio shows all your bets, your current positions, and any winnings you can claim.

### Step 1 — Open your portfolio

Click **Portfolio** in the navigation menu (or your wallet address, then **Portfolio**).

![Step 1 — Open portfolio](./images/open-portfolio.png)

### Step 2 — Review your positions

You'll see a list of every market you've bet on, including:

- The market name and your chosen outcome
- How much you bet
- The current odds
- Whether the market is still open, resolved, or awaiting your claim

![Step 2 — Portfolio positions](./images/portfolio-positions.png)

### Step 3 — Check claimable winnings

Winnings from resolved markets you've won appear in a **Claimable** section. See [Section 6](#6-claiming-your-winnings) to collect them.

![Step 3 — Claimable winnings](./images/claimable-winnings.png)

## 6. Claiming Your Winnings

When a market you bet on resolves in your favor, you need to claim your winnings to move them into your wallet.

### Step 1 — Find a resolved market you won

Open your portfolio and look for markets marked **Resolved** where you picked the winning outcome. These will show a **Claim** button.

![Step 1 — Find claimable market](./images/find-claimable.png)

### Step 2 — Click "Claim Winnings"

Click the **Claim Winnings** button on the market, or use the **Claim All** button to collect everything at once.

![Step 2 — Claim button](./images/claim-button.png)

### Step 3 — Review the claim

A panel will show how much you're claiming. Review the amount and click **Confirm**.

![Step 3 — Review claim](./images/review-claim.png)

### Step 4 — Approve in Freighter

Approve the transaction in Freighter, just like when you placed a bet.

![Step 4 — Approve claim](./images/approve-claim.png)

### Step 5 — Confirm your winnings arrived

Once confirmed, your winnings will be sent to your wallet. You can verify the balance in Freighter or your portfolio.

![Step 5 — Winnings claimed](./images/winnings-claimed.png)

> 💡 **Tip:** You can claim winnings at any time — there's no rush. They'll stay available until you collect them.

## 7. Supported Networks

The app runs on the **Stellar** network. Which network you're connected to determines whether you're using real funds or test funds.

### Mainnet (real funds)

- **Network:** Stellar Mainnet (Public)
- **Use for:** Real betting with real funds.
- **Funds:** Real XLM and other Stellar assets.

### Testnet (test funds)

- **Network:** Stellar Testnet
- **Use for:** Practicing and trying out the app. Test funds have no real value.
- **Funds:** Free test XLM from the [Stellar Laboratory](https://laboratory.stellar.org/#account-creator?network=test).

### How to check which network you're on

The app shows your current network in the header. You can also check in Freighter: open the extension and look at the network selector at the top.

![Network indicator](./images/network-indicator.png)

> ⚠️ **Important:** Make sure your Freighter wallet is set to the **same network** as the app. If they don't match, transactions will fail. See [Troubleshooting](#9-troubleshooting).

## 8. FAQ

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. Your wallet is your account. Just connect Freighter and you're ready to go.

**Does the app hold my funds?**
No. Your funds always stay in your wallet. The app only asks you to sign transactions — it never takes custody of your money.

**Can I cancel a bet after placing it?**
No. Once a bet is confirmed on the network, it can't be cancelled. Review your bet carefully before approving.

**Why did my odds change after I placed my bet?**
Odds move as more people bet. The odds shown when you confirmed are the odds you locked in for that bet.

**When can I claim my winnings?**
As soon as the market resolves in your favor. Winnings stay claimable until you collect them.

**What happens if a market is cancelled?**
If a market is cancelled, your stake is typically returned and can be claimed from your portfolio.

**Which wallets are supported?**
Currently **Freighter** is supported. Support for other Stellar wallets may be added in the future.

**Do I need to pay fees?**
Yes — every transaction on Stellar requires a tiny network fee (a fraction of a cent). This is paid from your wallet.

**Can I use the app on my phone?**
The app works in mobile browsers, but Freighter is currently a desktop browser extension. For the best experience, use a desktop browser.

**How do I know if I'm on testnet or mainnet?**
Check the network indicator in the app header and the network selector in Freighter. They must match.

## 9. Troubleshooting

### Wallet issues

**"Connect Wallet" does nothing, or Freighter doesn't pop up**

- Make sure the Freighter extension is installed and **unlocked**.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Check that your browser allows pop-ups from the app.

**The app says my wallet is not connected, but Freighter shows I'm connected**

- Click **Connect Wallet** again to re-establish the connection.
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting.
- Refresh the page.

**I see the wrong wallet address**

- Freighter may have multiple accounts. Open Freighter, switch to the account you want to use, then reconnect in the app.

### Transaction issues

**My transaction was rejected**

- You may have clicked **Reject** in Freighter. Try again and click **Approve**.
- Make sure you have enough funds to cover the bet amount plus the network fee.

**My transaction is stuck or "pending" for a long time**

- Stellar transactions usually confirm in a few seconds. If it's taking longer, wait a minute and refresh the page.
- Check the network status at [status.stellar.org](https://status.stellar.org/).

**I got an "insufficient balance" error**

- You don't have enough funds in your wallet to cover the bet plus the fee. Add funds or reduce your bet amount.

**I placed a bet but don't see it in my portfolio**

- Refresh the page. It can take a few seconds for the network to confirm and the app to update.
- Make sure you're looking at the correct network (testnet vs. mainnet).

### Network issues

**Transactions fail with a network error**

- Your Freighter wallet and the app must be on the **same network**. Open Freighter and check the network selector — it should match the network shown in the app header.
- If you're on testnet, make sure you have test funds from the [Stellar Laboratory](https://laboratory.stellar.org/#account-creator?network=test).

**The app won't load or shows a connection error**

- Check your internet connection.
- Try refreshing the page or using a different browser.
- Check the network status at [status.stellar.org](https://status.stellar.org/).

**I can't claim my winnings**

- Make sure the market has actually resolved. Unresolved markets can't be claimed.
- Confirm you picked the winning outcome.
- Make sure you're on the same network where you placed the bet.
